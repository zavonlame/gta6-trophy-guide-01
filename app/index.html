    Roadmap to the platinum

    Six stages, in order. Story first — it unlocks the map, the activities and the contacts everything else needs. Story missions can be replayed, so no chapter trophy can be missed. The six trophies flagged as missable are in stage 2 and stage 5: do those when they come up, not later.

      Missable trophies

      Six trophies read as one-time moments in their official descriptions, so this guide treats them as missable. Each one says so plainly, and none of them is confirmed in the game yet — the game launched days ago and these calls are our reading of the official descriptions. Everything else on the list can be earned after the story, at your own pace.

      Story missions themselves are replayable: chapter and mission trophies cannot be missed, and neither can anything tied to an activity, a collectible or a shop that stays open.
